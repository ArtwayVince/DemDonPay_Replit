---
name: Community organization trust
description: Product rules for newly submitted organizations and representative-role claims.
---

Newly submitted organizations should appear immediately in Dem Don Pay and be visibly marked unverified. Reports can identify the reporter as an employee or organization representative, but representative claims are self-reported and must not be presented as verified without an actual verification step.

**Why:** the user chose immediate listing, while distinguishing genuine organizational representatives from employees still requires trust signals.

**How to apply:** keep newly added organizations visibly unverified, label representative roles as self-reported, and require a real verification workflow before showing an official/verified representative badge.
